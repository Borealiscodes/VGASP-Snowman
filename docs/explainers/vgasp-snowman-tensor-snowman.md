🧊 Bill Nye Tile: “What’s a Tensor Snowman?”
Think of a Snowman as a stack of spheres living in a curved world.  
To keep it stable, predictable, and physically correct, we use tensors — math objects that describe how things bend, stretch, rotate, and melt.

- Invariant Tensor → keeps the Snowman from breaking physics  
- Curvature Tensor → tells us how round, squishy, or stable each part is  
- Holonomy Tensor → tracks how the Snowman’s orientation changes when it rolls  
- Dissipation Tensor → governs melting, deformation, and repair  

🧪 Tensors = the rules of the Snowman universe.

🧩 1. Overview
The Snowman world‑model is a tensor‑structured geometric system.  
Every operation — roll, stack, decorate, melt, repair — is governed by tensor fields that encode:

- invariants  
- curvature  
- holonomy  
- dissipation  
- allowable transformations  

This explainer introduces the tensor geometry underlying Snowman v2.0.

---

🏔️ 2. The Snowman Manifold
Snowman lives on a 3‑layer manifold:

1. Base manifold — the winter meadow (global geometry)  
2. Object manifold — the Snowman body (local geometry)  
3. Operator manifold — the transformation space (governance geometry)

Each layer has its own tensor fields.

---

🔢 3. Tensor Fields

🎯 3.1. Invariant Tensor \( I_{ij} \)
Encodes what must remain unchanged under all valid Snowman operations.

Examples:

- mass distribution  
- center‑of‑gravity bounds  
- curvature thresholds  
- symmetry constraints  

Referenced by:

- roll operator  
- stack operator  
- banned‑constructs governance  

---

🌀 3.2. Curvature Tensor \( K_{ij} \)
Defines the curvature field of the Snowman body.

Determines:

- stability  
- holonomy  
- allowable roll trajectories  
- deformation limits  

Curvature governs whether a Snowman can:

- roll downhill  
- roll uphill  
- maintain shape  
- collapse  

---

🔄 3.3. Holonomy Tensor \( H_{ijk} \)
Tracks how orientation changes when Snowman moves through curved space.

Determines:

- deterministic steering  
- operator ordering  
- roll → stack → decorate sequencing  

Essential for:

- operator ordering explainer  
- dissipation rules  
- invariant surfaces  

---

🔥 3.4. Dissipation Tensor \( D_{ij} \)
Encodes structured decay:

- melting  
- deformation  
- entropy  
- repair cost  

Ensures Snowman behaves physically, not magically.

---

🛠️ 4. Operator Interaction
Each Snowman operator interacts with the tensor fields:

| Operator | Tensor Interaction |
|---------|---------------------|
| roll | \( I{ij}, K{ij}, H_{ijk} \) |
| stack | \( I{ij}, K{ij} \) |
| decorate | \( I_{ij} \) |
| melt | \( D_{ij} \) |
| repair | \( D{ij}, I{ij} \) |

This is the backbone of deterministic Snowman governance.
