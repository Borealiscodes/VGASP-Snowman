## 1. A Kind Reminder: Silicon Is Connected to the Earth

Accessible software distribution and open research toolchains are worthy goals—**provided we never pretend that compute floats free of the physical planet**. Every accelerator, server cluster, and edge device rests on rare-earth mining, ultrapure water consumption, high-grade grid energy, and eventual e-waste disposal (`10.5281/zenodo.23246401`).

True **Dignity Under Constraint** honors both human researchers and ecological limits by favoring portable, low-footprint, zero-bloat software that extends the useful life of existing hardware rather than encouraging unbounded chip churn.
